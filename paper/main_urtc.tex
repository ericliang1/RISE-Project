\title{Reaching the Actionable Radius:\\
Physics-Guided Calibrated Localization of Methane Sources\\
under Wind Uncertainty}

\author{\IEEEauthorblockN{Eric Liang}
\IEEEauthorblockA{Department of Computer Science, Boston University\\
eric.jinchen.liang@gmail.com}}

\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Calibrated machine-learning localizers promise guaranteed search regions for
methane super-emitters, but a coverage guarantee says nothing about
usefulness. On a methane testbed whose closed-form physics makes the
\emph{exact} Bayesian posterior computable, we audit what calibration is
worth: a conformal-wrapped deep localizer with a verified 90\% guarantee
delivers a 94--98\,m median search radius where the data support 6--7\,m.
Controlled elimination traces the gap to a single missing classical
computation --- per-cell matched filtering --- and supplying it as
\emph{input channels} to a 0.2\%-parameter zero-initialized head brings
three architecture families into a 1.5\,m band at 46--48\,m, at the 50\,m
scale used for facility association in EPA super-emitter notifications. We
then make the setting honest: the wind is a \emph{measurement}
(10$^\circ$/10\% per-step error), never the truth. Exact plug-in inversion
retains its guarantee only by returning essentially the entire site, while
one unified recipe --- physics maps parameterized by the declared wind-error
budget, provably reducing to the classical matched filter at zero error ---
degrades gracefully: 82--84\,m ignoring the budget, 74--76\,m with
evidence moments, and \textbf{70--73\,m} with a wind-marginalized
goodness-of-fit channel, at unchanged coverage and millisecond inference.
Accuracy under wind uncertainty is set by how much of the marginalization
the inputs carry: show the network what the physics computes ---
and what the physics is unsure of.
\end{abstract}

\begin{IEEEkeywords}
methane monitoring, conformal prediction, simulation-based inference,
wind uncertainty, physics-guided machine learning
\end{IEEEkeywords}

\section{Introduction}
Methane delivers roughly $80\times$ the 20-year warming potency of CO$_2$
and decays within about a decade, so rapid repair of oil-and-gas
\emph{super-emitters} is among the highest-leverage near-term climate
actions. Detection has advanced rapidly --- UNEP's satellite alert system
issued over a thousand notifications in its first two years \cite{unep} ---
and attribution is the downstream bottleneck between detection and operator
investigation. Regulation supplies a concrete scale: EPA super-emitter
notifications associate alerts with facilities at a 50\,m radius \cite{epa}
(program implementation currently extended to 2027), with analogous duties
in the EU \cite{eu}. We adopt 50\,m as an operationally motivated,
facility-scale localization target --- a region that triggers a focused
investigation, not yet a repair.

Continuous monitoring with sparse fixed sensor networks is where such
localization can happen: a handful of cheap masts, always on, catching the
plume under naturally meandering wind. Machine learning supplies millisecond
inference, and split conformal prediction \cite{conformal} supplies a
guarantee: a region provably containing the source 90\% of the time.
\textbf{The gap is twofold.} First, coverage guarantees frequency, not
usefulness: no standard diagnostic \cite{sbc} can measure how far a
calibrated region sits from the smallest region the data support. Second,
the classical alternative --- exact inversion of the dispersion physics ---
assumes the wind is \emph{known}, while every real deployment has only a
wind \emph{measurement}, and behavior under that mismatch was largely
uncharacterized.

\textbf{Contributions.}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{C1 --- An exact-posterior audit.} A methane testbed (CH4-T)
whose closed-form physics makes the true posterior of every scenario
computable, so conformal \emph{efficiency} can be measured in meters against
the information floor (6.2\,m at matched coverage). Finding: a verified-90\%
deep localizer delivers 94--98\,m --- calibrated is not actionable.
\item \textbf{C2 --- A diagnosis by elimination.} Controlled probes --- three
encoder families, a strengthened flow-based neural posterior estimator, and
the conformalized classical statistic alone --- convict a single missing
computation, per-cell matched filtering, best supplied as \emph{input
channels}: two closed-form maps and a zero-initialized 0.2\%-parameter head
bring all encoders to 46--48\,m with ordinary label training.
\item \textbf{C3 --- One budget-aware recipe that survives measured wind.}
The same maps, widened to carry a declared wind-error budget $\varepsilon$
(evidence moments and a wind-marginalized misfit channel over the error
model), provably reduce to the classical matched filter at
$\varepsilon{=}0$ and remain calibrated at 70--73\,m under a realistic
10$^\circ$/10\% budget --- where exact inversion built on the same physics
returns essentially the entire site.
\end{itemize}
The components are deliberately classical --- matched filters are 1940s
detection theory; conformal prediction and label training are standard ---
and the composition is minimal and fully ablated (Appendix). The exact
posterior's role throughout is measurement, not supervision.

\section{Related Work}
\textbf{Conformal prediction} provides distribution-free coverage
\cite{conformal}; efficiency is the open half of the problem, and we
contribute an audit that scores it against the exact Bayesian reference at
matched, verified coverage. \textbf{Simulation-based inference} amortizes
posteriors with neural density estimators \cite{npe,nsf} at budgets matching
ours \cite{sbibm}; its diagnostics test calibration \cite{sbc}, and a
growing literature studies robustness when the simulator's nuisance
parameters are misspecified at deployment \cite{robsbi} --- our
measured-wind design is exactly that setting, addressed by building the
declared error model into the inputs. Our maps are \emph{hand-derived
sufficient statistics} supplied as spatial channels, a deliberate,
auditable counterpoint to learned summaries. \textbf{Source-term
estimation} inverts dispersion models via optimization or MCMC at
minutes-to-hours per event \cite{newman}; deep localizers give millisecond
inference but lacked instruments certifying how much information they
extract --- or how they fail when the wind is mismeasured.
\textbf{Controlled-release evaluation} (METEC) scores continuous-monitoring
systems on detection, quantification, and equipment-scale localization
\cite{metec}; CH4-T mirrors that task structure at super-emitter rates with
exact ground truth, complementing --- not replacing --- field validation.

\section{Methodology}
\subsection{Testbed, measured wind, and the audit (C1)}
\textbf{CH4-T} places a source of unknown location and rate
(10--500\,kg\,h$^{-1}$, log-uniform) on a $500{\times}500$\,m site sensed by
4--12 masts (ppm readings, 0.2--3\,ppm noise, dropout). The plume is a
steady Gaussian under Pasquill--Gifford stability with Briggs open-country
dispersion (coefficients verified against published tables; mass flux
conserved to $4{\times}10^{-6}$), driven quasi-statically by a fluctuating
wind record (1--8\,m\,s$^{-1}$, AR(1) meander; 1-min steps, a 30-min
window). \textbf{Wind uncertainty is part of the simulator}: readings are
generated under the true wind, but the dataset records only the
\emph{measured} wind --- truth corrupted by 10$^\circ$ direction and 10\%
speed error per step, the accuracy class of inexpensive continuous-monitoring
anemometry --- and no model, at any stage, sees the truth. Because the
response is closed-form and linear in rate, the exact posterior over a
$64{\times}64$ grid is computable per scenario under known wind (adaptive
rate quadrature, float64, normalization error $\sim$$10^{-12}$), giving the
information floor: 7.0\,m at nominal 90\% (empirical 0.95), 6.2\,m at
matched empirical 0.89. A blocking gate requires conformal prediction
applied to the exact posterior itself to self-calibrate --- a gate that
caught two implementation bugs, including a tie-handling error at
degenerate scores whose fix we report for reuse. All models share identical
data (10k/1k/2k/2k splits, the modal SBI budget \cite{sbibm}), D4
augmentation, and an identical randomized-HPD split-conformal wrapper,
calibrated under the same wind condition it is evaluated in. Regions are
reported as equal-area circle radii.

\subsection{Diagnosis by elimination (C2)}
A DeepSets localizer trained on smoothed one-hot labels is calibrated
(coverage 0.89) at 94--98\,m --- the information for 6--7\,m is in the
readings, waiting to be extracted. \emph{Is it the estimator head?} A
representative flow-based neural posterior estimator on the same encoder ---
strengthened after adversarial review of our own implementation
($2.5\times$ budget, widened head, full-band conditioning) --- reaches
62.8--71.2\,m, and we verify its spline head can express 4.4\,m regions, so
the remaining blur is not head-structural. \emph{Is the classical statistic
enough alone?} Conformalizing the matched-filter map with no network
returns essentially the entire site: overconfident-wrong, so calibration
must be learned. \emph{Is the computation the missing piece?} Localization
requires correlating each candidate cell's space--time signature against
all readings --- per-cell matched filtering --- which pooled set encoders
did not recover under the tested architectures and budgets; supplying
exactly this computation as input (\S\ref{sec:rec}) collapses the gap on
every encoder. The network cannot compute what the statistic knows; the
statistic cannot calibrate what the network learns; the combination is the
method.

\subsection{One budget-aware recipe (C3)}
\label{sec:rec}
For any base localizer (Fig.~\ref{fig:pipeline}), with $g_c$ the unit-rate
response of cell $c$ \emph{at the measured wind} and
$a_c{=}\sum g_c y$, $b_c{=}\sum g_c^2$ the linear-Gaussian sufficient
statistics: \textbf{(1)}~declare the wind-error budget $\varepsilon$ (from
the anemometer specification), draw $K{=}8$ plausible true winds from the
error model centered on the measurement, and feed four $64{\times}64$
channels --- the \emph{mean} matched-filter statistic
$\bar z_c$, its \emph{spread} across draws (per-cell wind-fragility of the
evidence), mean sensitivity $\overline{\log b_c}$, and the
\emph{wind-marginalized misfit}
$\bar R_c = \overline{yy - 2\hat q_c a_c + \hat q_c^2 b_c}$,
$\hat q_c{=}\max(0, a_c/b_c)$ (median-normalized): the residual of the best
nonnegative rate fit, averaged over the winds the error model deems
plausible. All channels are closed-form linear algebra, milliseconds per
scenario, computed from the network's own inputs.
\textbf{(2)}~add a 3-layer zero-initialized convolutional head ($\sim$10k
parameters, 0.2\% of the base) summed with the localizer's logits, so at
step~0 the model \emph{is} the unmodified baseline and every comparison is
controlled by construction; \textbf{(3)}~train with ordinary smoothed-label
cross-entropy --- physics enters \emph{only} through the inputs;
\textbf{(4)}~wrap with split conformal calibrated under the deployment
condition. \textbf{At $\varepsilon{=}0$ the recipe provably reduces to the
classical matched filter}: the spread channel is identically zero, and at a
single wind the misfit obeys $R_c = yy - \sigma^2\max(z_c,0)^2$ --- a
per-scenario monotone transform of $z_c$ carrying no extra information ---
leaving exactly the two classical channels $(z_c, \log b_c)$. One recipe,
continuous in the distance from perfect wind; clean-wind results below are
its $\varepsilon{=}0$ instantiation.

\textbf{Why not invert the physics directly?} On this testbed one can ---
the exact posterior is our measuring instrument. In deployment, realistic
dispersion has no closed form and simulator inversion costs
minutes-to-hours per event \cite{newman} where monitoring needs seconds ---
and, as \S\ref{sec:robust} shows, plug-in inversion is brittle under
wind-measurement error in a way the learned recipe is not.

\subsection{The audit: calibrated is not actionable}

Both learned rows pass every calibration check; only the audit exposes that
one of them discards an order of magnitude of sharpness
(Table~\ref{tab:audit}). Two input channels and a 0.2\%-parameter head
close half the gap: a 50.3\% median-radius reduction (bootstrap 95\% CI
[48.9, 52.0]; 96.4\% of scenarios improved), with 51\% of individual
regions below the 50\,m facility scale (percentiles p50/p75/p90:
49/112/170\,m --- the median case reaches the scale; the upper tail defines
the remaining challenge).

Without the maps, encoder choice spans 40\,m; with them, all nine seed-runs
of three architecture families land inside a 1.5\,m band
(Table~\ref{tab:est}) --- the gains are available to whatever architecture a
practitioner already has, and encoder design ceases to be the binding
constraint. The flow head is a genuine lever without physics yet does not
close the gap, and the maps transfer onto it; the classical statistic
without a network is overconfident-wrong and pays with full-site regions.
Every rival explanation fails; the computation was missing, and inputs are
where it belongs.

\label{sec:robust}

Real anemometers err. Under the 10$^\circ$/10\% budget, plug-in inversion
at the measured wind assigns numerically negligible posterior mass to the
true source in 30\% of scenarios; split conformal preserves its guarantee
exactly as theory requires --- by returning essentially the entire site
(Table~\ref{tab:wind}). A tuned global noise-inflation variant does not
help: per-step wind error is not absorbable by a scalar noise scale. The
learned recipe, by contrast, degrades gracefully, and its accuracy climbs
monotonically with how much of the wind-error structure the maps carry:
evidence at the reported wind alone, 82--84\,m; adding the error-model's
evidence moments (mean and fragility), 74--76\,m; adding the marginalized
misfit channel, \textbf{70--73\,m} with a quarter of scenarios below
50\,m --- same network, same training, same guarantee, one forward pass.
The decomposition to remember: $94{\to}47$\,m is the price of a missing
computation, fixed by inputs; $47{\to}71$\,m is the price of wind
uncertainty at this budget, paid at the information level;
$108{\to}71$\,m is what physics inputs are worth when the wind is real.
The mechanism is constructive: because the physics --- and its declared
error model --- live in the input representation, clean labels give the
network thousands of supervised opportunities to learn the error's
systematic structure and weigh each cell's evidence accordingly.

\section{Limitations}
CH4-T is an instrument, not a deployment: single steady sources (no
zero-leak or multi-source scenarios), flat terrain without equipment wakes,
known stability class, quasi-static stepping, in-distribution marginal
coverage, and a 64$\times$64 grid whose $\sim$7.8\,m cells sit near the
oracle reference scale (a grid-refinement study is pending). The 6--7\,m
floor is a property of CH4-T's generative assumptions, not an atmospheric
limit, and it exists only under known wind: a wind-marginalized exact
reference is intractable here, so the measured-wind information floor is
unknown. The wind-error budget is assumed known and correctly specified
(instrument-specification information); sensitivity to a misspecified
budget is future work. Half of individual clean-wind cases still exceed
50\,m (p90 $=$ 170\,m), performance is mildly worse at the highest rates,
and architectural claims are scoped to tested encoders and budgets. Field
claims await controlled-release validation --- comparisons here are exact
regardless, because all models share one verified simulator.

\section{Conclusions}
On a testbed with exact Bayesian ground truth, a calibrated deep localizer
delivered a tenth of the sharpness its data support; controlled elimination
traced the gap to a single classical computation, and supplying it as input
channels brought three architectures into a 1.5\,m band at the 50\,m
facility scale. Made honest about wind --- a measurement, never the truth
--- exact inversion built on the same physics collapsed to the full site,
while one budget-aware recipe, provably classical at zero error, held its
guarantee at 70--73\,m, its accuracy set by how much of the wind
marginalization its inputs carry. The constructive message: simulators make
exact posteriors available as \emph{instruments} for auditing what learned
systems extract; classical signal processing still knows computations
networks need not rediscover; and under sensor uncertainty, the place to
put physics --- including its own error model --- is the input. We hope the
evidence standard --- exact-posterior audits, blocking self-calibration
gates, one-change-at-a-time controls --- travels furthest.
