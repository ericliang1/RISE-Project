\title{Reaching the Actionable Radius:\\
Physics-Guided Calibrated Localization of Methane Sources}

\author{\IEEEauthorblockN{Eric Liang}
\IEEEauthorblockA{Department of Computer Science, Boston University\\
eric.jinchen.liang@gmail.com}}

\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
We present a simple recipe that brings calibrated machine-learning
localization of high-rate methane sources to the 50\,m scale used for
facility association in EPA super-emitter notifications. On a
methane-configured testbed whose closed-form physics makes the \emph{exact}
Bayesian posterior computable for every scenario, we first contribute an
audit that measures, in meters, how much of the available information a
calibrated localizer delivers: a conformal-wrapped deep localizer with a
verified 90\% coverage guarantee attains a 94\,m median search radius where
the exact posterior supports 6--7\,m. Controlled elimination locates the
cause: not the information, not the estimator head (a strengthened
flow-based neural posterior estimator reaches only 63--71\,m), and not the
classical physics statistic alone (conformalized, it returns essentially the
entire site) --- but the absence of a classical computation, per-cell matched
filtering, that pooled set encoders did not recover under the tested
architectures and budgets. Supplying it directly --- two closed-form
matched-filter maps as input channels and a zero-initialized residual head,
0.2\% extra parameters, ordinary label training --- brings three
architecture families into a 1.5\,m band at 46--48\,m median radius
(a 50\% median radius reduction; 96\% of scenarios improved) at unchanged
coverage and millisecond latency. Under realistic wind-measurement error,
exact plug-in inversion retains its guarantee only by returning essentially
the entire site, while the same recipe remains calibrated at 75--85\,m, with
uncertainty-aware input maps recovering a further $\sim$8\,m. Calibrated,
physics-guided learning is both sharper and more robust than inversion built
on the same physics.
\end{abstract}

methane monitoring, conformal prediction, simulation-based inference,

\section{Introduction}
Methane delivers roughly $80\times$ the 20-year warming potency of CO$_2$
and decays within about a decade, so rapid repair of oil-and-gas
\emph{super-emitters} is among the highest-leverage near-term climate
actions. Detection has advanced rapidly --- UNEP's satellite alert system
issued over a thousand notifications in its first two years \cite{unep} ---
and attribution is a critical downstream bottleneck between detection and
operator investigation. Regulation suggests a concrete scale: EPA
super-emitter notifications associate alerts with facilities at a 50\,m
radius \cite{epa} (program implementation currently extended to 2027), with
analogous source-level duties in the EU \cite{eu}. We adopt 50\,m as an
operationally motivated, facility-scale localization target --- a region that
triggers a focused investigation, not yet a repair.

Continuous monitoring with sparse fixed point-sensor networks is where such
localization can happen: a handful of cheap masts around a facility, always
on, catching the plume as it sweeps across them under naturally meandering
wind. Machine learning supplies millisecond inference, and split conformal
prediction \cite{conformal} supplies a guarantee: a region provably
containing the source 90\% of the time. \textbf{The gap:} coverage
guarantees frequency, not usefulness. Two questions had no quantitative
answer: (i)~how close is a calibrated region to the smallest region the
sensor data support under the modeling assumptions, and (ii)~what mechanism
limits it, and what closes it? Standard diagnostics for amortized inference
test whether a posterior is calibrated \cite{sbc}; they cannot measure its
distance from the exact posterior at matched calibration.

\textbf{Contributions.}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{C1 --- An exact-posterior audit.} A methane testbed (CH4-T)
whose closed-form physics makes the true Bayesian posterior of every
scenario computable, so conformal efficiency can be measured in meters
against a model-based information reference (6.2\,m at matched empirical
coverage) and the 50\,m facility scale.
\item \textbf{C2 --- A diagnosis by elimination.} Controlled comparisons ---
a labels-only baseline, a strengthened flow-based neural posterior
estimator, and the conformalized classical statistic without any network ---
isolate the dominant bottleneck in our experiments: a classical computation
(per-cell matched filtering) that pooled encoders did not recover, and that
neither a modern estimator head nor the raw physics statistic supplies
alone.
\item \textbf{C3 --- A minimal recipe that reaches the scale.} Two
closed-form matched-filter maps plus a zero-initialized residual head
(0.2\% extra parameters, ordinary label training) bring three architecture
families to 46--48\,m median radius at unchanged coverage and latency, and
remain calibrated under wind-measurement error that reduces exact plug-in
inversion to site-sized regions.
\end{itemize}
The components are deliberately classical --- matched filters are 1940s
detection theory; conformal prediction and label training are standard ---
and the composition is minimal: the ablation record (Appendix) shows both
map channels contribute and neither the network nor the statistic suffices
alone. The exact posterior's role throughout is measurement, not
supervision.

\section{Related Work}
\textbf{Conformal prediction} provides distribution-free coverage
\cite{conformal}; efficiency is the open half of the problem, and we
contribute an audit that scores it against the exact Bayesian reference at
matched, verified coverage. \textbf{Simulation-based inference} amortizes
posteriors with neural density estimators \cite{npe,nsf} at simulation
budgets matching ours \cite{sbibm}; its diagnostics test calibration
\cite{sbc}, and our audit adds the efficiency question while placing a
representative flow-based estimator inside the comparison.
\textbf{Source-term estimation} inverts dispersion models via optimization
or MCMC at minutes-to-hours per event \cite{newman}; deep localizers give
millisecond inference but lacked instruments certifying how much information
they extract. \textbf{Physics-guided ML} injects domain structure through
features or architectures; our input maps are the forward model's
sufficient statistics under CH4-T assumptions --- classical matched-filter
detection theory supplied as spatial channels aligned with the output grid.
\textbf{Controlled-release evaluation} (METEC) scores continuous-monitoring
systems on detection, quantification, and equipment-scale localization
\cite{metec}; CH4-T mirrors that task structure at super-emitter rates with
exact ground truth, complementing --- not replacing --- field validation.

\section{Methodology}
\subsection{Testbed and exact-posterior audit (C1)}
\textbf{CH4-T} places a source of unknown location and rate
(10--500\,kg\,h$^{-1}$, log-uniform, bracketing the 100\,kg\,h$^{-1}$
super-emitter threshold) on a $500{\times}500$\,m site sensed by 4--12 masts
(ppm readings, 0.2--3\,ppm noise, dropout). The plume is a steady Gaussian
under known Pasquill--Gifford stability with Briggs open-country dispersion
(coefficients verified against published tables; mass flux conserved to
$4{\times}10^{-6}$), driven quasi-statically by a known fluctuating wind
record (1--8\,m\,s$^{-1}$, AR(1) meander; steps read as 1-min averages, a
30-min window). Because the response is closed-form and linear in rate, the
exact posterior over a $64{\times}64$ grid is computable per scenario
(adaptive rate quadrature, float64, normalization error $\sim$$10^{-12}$).
A blocking gate requires conformal prediction applied to the exact posterior
itself to self-calibrate --- a gate that caught two implementation bugs,
including a tie-handling error at degenerate scores whose fix we report for
reuse. All models share identical data (10k/1k/2k/2k splits, the modal SBI
budget \cite{sbibm}), D4 symmetry augmentation, and an identical
randomized-HPD split-conformal wrapper at verified 90\% coverage. Regions
are reported as equal-area circle radii; the exact-posterior reference is
7.0\,m at nominal 90\% (empirical 0.95) and 6.2\,m at matched empirical
0.89.

\subsection{Diagnosis by elimination (C2)}
A DeepSets localizer trained on smoothed one-hot labels is calibrated
(coverage 0.89) at a 94\,m median radius --- the information for 6--7\,m is
in the readings, waiting to be extracted. Three controlled probes isolate
why. \emph{Is it the estimator head?} A representative flow-based neural
posterior estimator on the same encoder --- strengthened after adversarial
review of our own implementation ($2.5\times$ budget, widened head,
full-band conditioning) --- reaches 62.8--71.2\,m, and we verify its spline
head can express 4.4\,m regions, so its remaining blur is not
head-structural. \emph{Is the classical physics statistic enough by
itself?} Conformalizing the normalized matched-filter map with no network
returns essentially the entire site: the statistic is overconfident-wrong,
and calibration must be learned. \emph{Is the computation the missing
piece?} Localization requires correlating each candidate cell's space--time
signature against all readings --- per-cell matched filtering --- which
pooled set representations did not recover under the tested architectures
and budgets; supplying exactly this computation as input (\S\ref{sec:rec})
collapses the gap on every encoder. Together: the network cannot compute
what the statistic knows; the statistic cannot calibrate what the network
learns; the combination is the method.

\subsection{The recipe (C3)}
\label{sec:rec}
For any base localizer (Fig.~\ref{fig:pipeline}): \textbf{(1)}~compute per
cell $c$ the sufficient statistics $a_c{=}\sum g_c y$, $b_c{=}\sum g_c^2$ of
the linear-Gaussian model ($g_c$ the unit-rate response) and feed two
$64{\times}64$ channels: the matched-filter statistic
$z_c{=}a_c/(\sigma\sqrt{b_c})$ and $\log b_c$ --- milliseconds, closed form,
computed from the network's own inputs (no new information, only a
computation); \textbf{(2)}~add a 3-layer zero-initialized convolutional head
($\sim$10k parameters, 0.2\% of the base) summed with the localizer's
logits, so at step~0 the model \emph{is} the unmodified baseline and every
comparison is controlled by construction; \textbf{(3)}~train with ordinary
smoothed-label cross-entropy; \textbf{(4)}~wrap with unchanged split
conformal. Physics computes the evidence; the network weighs it honestly;
conformal certifies the result.

\textbf{Why not invert the physics directly?} On this testbed one can ---
the exact posterior is our measuring instrument, tractable by construction.
In deployment, realistic dispersion has no closed form and simulator
inversion costs minutes-to-hours per event \cite{newman} where monitoring
needs seconds --- and, as \S\ref{sec:robust} shows, plug-in inversion is
brittle under wind-model misspecification in a way the learned recipe is
not.

The maps improve every architecture, and the improvement is uniform to a
striking degree: without them, encoder choice spans 40\,m (94 vs.\ 55);
with them, all nine seed-runs land inside a 1.5\,m band
(Table~\ref{tab:main}) --- the gains are available to whatever architecture
a practitioner already has, and encoder design ceases to be the binding
constraint. Against the labels-only baseline the median radius reduction is
50.3\% (bootstrap 95\% CI [48.9, 52.0]; 96.4\% of scenarios improved).
Point accuracy is essentially unchanged: the maps improve the \emph{shape of
uncertainty}, which region size measures. Operationally: 52\% of individual
test cases fall below 50\,m (median 47\,m); the 75th/90th/95th percentiles
are 86/131/146\,m; performance is mildly worse at the highest rates
(56.9\,m median at 250--500\,kg\,h$^{-1}$, vs.\ 42--43\,m below
100\,kg\,h$^{-1}$) --- the median case reaches the facility scale, and the
upper tail defines the remaining challenge.

The flow head is a genuine lever --- it improves markedly on the categorical
labels-only baseline with no physics at all --- and the maps transfer onto
it (Table~\ref{tab:flow}). The convolutional head reading the maps
\emph{spatially} extracts more than the flow's parametric conditioning at
identical information. Our approach is compared against, and complements, a
modern estimator rather than being superseded by it.

\label{sec:robust}
\begin{table}[t]
\centering

speed); training, calibration and test all under the same error
distribution; 3 seeds for learned rows.}
\label{tab:wind}
\begin{tabular}{lcc}
\toprule
Pipeline & Coverage & Radius \\
\midrule
Plug-in inversion (measured wind) & 1.00 & 282\,m (full site) \\
Plug-in, inflated-noise robust variant & 1.00 & 282\,m \\
Ours, deterministic maps & 0.91--0.92 & 81.7--84.9\,m \\
\textbf{Ours, ensemble maps} & 0.89--0.90 & \textbf{74.7--75.4\,m} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

Real anemometers err. Under a 10$^\circ$/10\% observation error, plug-in
inversion at the measured wind assigns numerically negligible (below
float64) posterior mass to the true source in 30\% of scenarios; split
conformal preserves its guarantee exactly as theory requires --- by
returning essentially the entire site (Table~\ref{tab:wind}). A global
noise-inflation robust variant, tuned on calibration data, does not help:
per-step wind error is not absorbable by a scalar noise scale. The recipe,
trained through the same error, remains calibrated at useful sizes, and
\emph{ensemble maps} --- the mean and spread of the matched-filter maps over
eight draws from the wind-error model --- recover a further $\sim$8\,m.
Under the modeled wind-measurement error, learned physics-guided
localization is more robust than plug-in inversion built on the same
physics; a wind-marginalized exact reference is future work.

\section{Limitations}
CH4-T is an instrument, not a deployment: single steady sources (no
zero-leak or multi-source scenarios), flat terrain without equipment wakes,
known stability class, quasi-static stepping, in-distribution marginal
coverage, and a 64$\times$64 grid whose $\sim$7.8\,m cells sit near the
oracle reference scale (a grid-refinement study is pending). The 6--7\,m
reference is a property of CH4-T's generative assumptions, not an
atmospheric limit. Half of individual cases still exceed 50\,m, and the
upper tail (p90 $=$ 131\,m) is unresolved. The architectural claim is
scoped to tested encoders and budgets. Field claims await
controlled-release validation --- comparisons here are exact regardless,
because all models share one verified simulator.

\section{Conclusions}
On a testbed with exact Bayesian ground truth, a calibrated deep localizer
delivered a tenth of the sharpness its sensor data support; controlled
elimination traced the gap to a single classical computation, and supplying
that computation --- two matched-filter maps and a 0.2\%-parameter
zero-initialized head, ordinary labels, nothing else --- brought three
architectures into a 1.5\,m band at the 50\,m facility-association scale,
halving the median search radius at unchanged cost, ahead of a strengthened
flow-based estimator, and calibrated under wind error that reduces exact
inversion to site-sized regions. The constructive message: simulators make
exact posteriors available as \emph{instruments} for auditing what learned
systems extract, and classical signal processing still knows computations
that networks need not rediscover. We hope the evidence standard ---
exact-posterior audits, blocking self-calibration gates,
one-change-at-a-time controls --- travels furthest.
